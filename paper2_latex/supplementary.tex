\section{Scope and frozen evidence boundary}

This Supporting Information accompanies the two-stage ADMET reliability study reported in the main manuscript. Stage~I is the frozen four-endpoint diagnostic audit. Development splits and seed~99 were excluded from its scientific conclusions. Random and scaffold analyses used confirmatory seeds 101--110, and similarity-cluster analyses used seeds 101--105. Model families were not treated as independent inferential replicates. Method contrasts were paired within the same endpoint, split, seed, model, and classification imbalance regime.

Stage~II evaluates TAME under the prospectively separated RACER-C4 protocol. The public Tox21 leaderboard batch and seeds 101--105 were used for architecture development only. The independent EPA batch used fresh seeds 211--215. The executable lock, method, protected label, primary endpoints, promotion checks, efficiency guardrail, final-label hash, and negative-result policy were fixed before final-label access. Final predictions and the label-blind transport audit were hashed into the promotion record before the locked label file was acquired or parsed.

The Stage~I tables below are generated directly from the versioned, integrity-checked manuscript result package. The generation step performs no model fitting, split construction, conformal threshold estimation, method selection, or additional hypothesis testing. Descriptive cross-model averages are used for compact endpoint- and split-level reporting. Where inferential stability is discussed, paired-effect tables report the number of frozen model-specific cross-seed 95\% confidence intervals that lie entirely above or below zero in the stated direction. The Stage~II tables are transcribed from the frozen publication report, endpoint summary, transport-audit summary, and exclusion record.

\section{Reproducibility and machine-readable files}

From the repository root, the Supporting Information tables and PDF are generated with:

\begin{verbatim}
conda activate aidd_paper
python paper2_admet_benchmark/scripts/36_build_clean_supporting_information.py
cd paper2_latex
latexmk -C supplementary.tex
latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -file-line-error -halt-on-error supplementary.tex
\end{verbatim}

The clean builder reads only the frozen CSV files under \path{paper2_admet_benchmark/results/manuscript_assets/}. It does not require local row-level prediction files, trained model binaries, or intermediate aggregation tables. Its post-processing is limited to LaTeX safety, readable display labels, landscape-page placement, and print formatting; it does not alter any numerical result. The machine-readable CSVs remain the authoritative source for full decimal precision, complete SHA-256 hashes, and secondary metrics. High-domain ClinTox subgroup results under scaffold and similarity-cluster splitting should be treated as descriptive because positive-class support can be sparse. No sparse or adverse result was removed after inspection.

The primary 95\% interval was $+0.5827$ to $+2.0051$ percentage points. It used 2000 hierarchical-bootstrap draws with seed~44021, resampling endpoints first and compounds within endpoint and true class second while retaining all five seeds. The point estimate was positive and the MacroCSY point estimate was inside the frozen $-5$-point efficiency guardrail. On the class-balanced MacroCSY scale, the guardrail permits at most five fewer correct singleton outputs per 100 cases. It was fixed after public development as an architecture-selection tolerance and before final-label access; it was not calibrated to clinical or regulatory utility. The executable lock retains the historical field name \texttt{macro\_csy\_noninferiority\_margin} and result code \texttt{coverage\_gain\_with\_efficiency\_noninferiority}. Because no MacroCSY confidence-interval criterion was prespecified, the manuscript does not claim formal non-inferiority. The lock also explicitly prohibited an arbitrary-shift coverage guarantee or automatic scientific-superiority claim.

All 60 final endpoint--seed cells completed. Across development and final evaluation, TAME had zero baseline-inclusion violations, zero newly created singletons, and zero emitted empty sets. Two final external structures failed the frozen RDKit standardizer; their public sample identities were retained with full-set exclusions. Standardized overlaps with the 10K training cohort were excluded separately by endpoint, yielding 308 recorded endpoint--structure overlap pairs.

A post-evaluation code audit found that the original percentile bootstrap iterated unique drawn endpoints through an unordered Python set. A hash-verified inference-only repair preserved first-occurrence order. It changed only the percentile interval, from the original sealed $[+0.5827,+1.9696]$ points to the deterministic publication interval $[+0.5827,+2.0051]$ points after rounding. Predictions, labels, metric tables, the $+1.3649$-point estimate, the $-1.6067$-point MacroCSY change, and the predeclared interpretation were unchanged. The original and repaired reports are both retained.
