\begin{abstract}
Aggregate accuracy and marginal coverage can conceal which chemical classes fail and whether uncertainty outputs remain informative under distribution shift. We used a two-stage, representation-controlled study to turn those diagnostic failures into an auditable conformal design. First, frozen ECFP-based predictors were evaluated on BBBP, ClinTox, ESOL, and Lipophilicity under repeated random, label-blind scaffold, and similarity-cluster splits. The audit combined point performance, probability calibration, applicability-domain analysis, marginal and class-conditional conformal prediction, shift weighting, and composition-aware selective prediction. Random splitting was generally optimistic; chemical similarity ranked risk for BBBP and Lipophilicity but was weak for ClinTox and reversed for scaffold-split ESOL. At nominal 90\% coverage, ClinTox marginal and shift-weighted procedures achieved overall coverage near 0.90 while positive-class coverage was only 0.068--0.158. Mondrian calibration restored positive-class coverage to approximately 0.93--0.94 but returned ambiguous two-label sets for about 71\% of compounds. These observations motivated TAME, a transport-audited multi-view envelope that activates two label-free weighting views only after prespecified diagnostics, always contains the ordinary Mondrian set, and falls back when transport evidence is inadequate. The candidate and a $-5$ percentage-point Macro correct-singleton yield (MacroCSY) non-inferiority bound were frozen on the public Tox21 development batch. In a single sealed evaluation on the independent EPA batch, six primary endpoints across five fresh seeds gave a $+1.3649$-point mean change in minimum-class coverage (hierarchical-bootstrap 95\% interval, $+0.5827$ to $+2.0051$) and a $-1.6067$-point MacroCSY change. Both approved views passed in all 60 endpoint--seed cells; the diagnostic ECFP view failed in all 60. TAME created neither empty sets nor new confident singletons. The evidence supports failure-aware, audited reliability improvement in this locked Tox21 setting, not an arbitrary-shift coverage guarantee or universal algorithmic superiority.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} ADMET prediction; conformal prediction; applicability domain; covariate shift; Tox21; transport audit
