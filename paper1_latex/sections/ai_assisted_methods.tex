\subsection{Use of AI-assisted tools}

OpenAI ChatGPT was used to assist with language editing, manuscript organization, code debugging, LaTeX formatting, figure-layout drafting, and reproducibility-material preparation. It was not used to generate or alter molecular data, endpoint labels, partition assignments, predictive outputs, statistical results, or scientific conclusions. All analyses were executed in the authors' controlled computational environment. The authors reviewed and edited all manuscript content and independently verified the references, code, numerical outputs, statistical analyses, and figures, and they take full responsibility for the submitted work.
